# Disagreement-weighted calibration — bounded pre-registration (2026-09-20)

Status: rejected by the preregistered local gate; no control or checkpoint was built.

## Product question

Can a second PTQ calibration round reduce Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 quality loss without changing
checkpoint size, checkpoint format, vLLM kernels, or serving speed?

This experiment is **quality-only**. It does not claim additional compression or acceleration.

## Hypothesis

Uniform calibration spends equal statistical weight on contexts where H64 already follows BF16 and
contexts where its output distribution diverges. Giving more Hessian weight to calibration
sequences with larger measured `KL(BF16 || H64)` will select better MXFP4 scales on held-out data.

The first probe is deliberately global and sequence-level. It does not attribute disagreement to a
layer, replay a suffix, differentiate through the model, or retain activations.

## Frozen DWC construction

1. Prepare the exact 64 H64 calibration sequences as an immutable token-ID manifest.
2. Collect the complete next-token distribution for BF16 and H64 at the final position of each
   sequence.
3. Compute one disagreement value per sequence:
   `d_i = KL(P_BF16,i || P_H64,i)`.
4. Set `w_i = softmax(d_i / temperature)` and normalize the weights to mean one.
5. Choose the temperature deterministically so importance-weight effective sample size is exactly
   50% of the 64 calibration sequences. There is no temperature sweep.
6. Recollect the block Hessians while applying `w_i` to every token of sequence `i`:
   `H = sum_i,t w_i x_i,t x_i,t^T / sum_i,t w_i`.
7. Keep H64's scale search, percentile, clipping depth, packing, policy, and source weights
   unchanged. Only the calibration aggregation weights differ.

Every weight artifact must contain the token hash, BF16/H64 distribution hashes, achieved effective
sample size, temperature, raw disagreements, and normalized weights. Calibration fails closed if
the token hash differs.

## Bounded local gate

No complete checkpoint is built initially.

- Probe layers: `8`, `16`, `42`, and `62`, gated MLP only.
- Candidate: DWC block-Hessian scales for `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, and `down_proj` together.
- Baseline: unchanged packed H64 layer.
- Held-out split A: offset `152`, `8×512` tokens.
- Held-out split B: offset `160`, `8×512` tokens.
- Primary metric: exact final BF16-teacher KL over the last eight positions per context after the
  unchanged packed H64 suffix.
- Runtime: BF16 replay with SDPA; identical implementation on both splits.
- Bootstrap: 100,000 paired resamples, seed `20260920`.

The local gate passes only if all of the following hold:

1. The packed H64 identities match the frozen checkpoint and every candidate recurrence residual is
   at most `1e-10`.
2. DWC lowers mean teacher KL on both splits for at least three of the four fixed layers.
3. Each passing layer wins at least five of eight paired contexts on each split.
4. The pooled paired 95% interval for `H64 KL - DWC KL`, stratified by layer and split, has a
   strictly positive lower bound.

Ties fail. Failure ends this DWC configuration. It does not authorize another effective-sample-size
target, temperature rule, layer set, or calibration corpus.

## Specific-signal control

If the local DWC gate passes, repeat only its passing layers with teacher-uncertainty weights at the
same effective sample size. DWC must remain better than both uniform H64 and this control before a
checkpoint build. This separates quantized-model disagreement from merely emphasizing intrinsically
difficult teacher contexts.

## Whole-model gate

Only a local-and-control pass permits one complete DWC checkpoint. Promotion then requires:

1. lower paired WikiText perplexity than H64;
2. lower mean full-vocabulary forward KL on the immutable 128-context divergence suite;
3. a strictly positive paired-bootstrap lower bound for `H64 KL - DWC KL`;
4. no reduction in BF16 top-1 agreement;
5. byte-identical quantization metadata and checkpoint size class apart from packed values;
6. GSM8K non-inferiority under the already frozen evaluation protocol.

Failure retains H64 and records a negative result. Publication and upload remain owner decisions.

## Observed result

The implementation first reproduced all 400 tensors in the original uniform H64 calibration
artifact bit for bit. DWC then changed all 400 block-Hessian tensors, with a median relative
Frobenius change of `3.05%`. Weighted collection used `8.11 GiB` peak host RSS and `3.28 GiB` peak
accelerator allocation.

The fixed ESS rule produced an achieved ESS of `31.9999993`, temperature `0.07763834`, minimum
weight `0.6117`, and maximum weight `6.8588`. Mean calibration-sequence
`KL(BF16 || H64)` was `0.0246771` nats.

The local gate failed:

| Layer | Offset 152: mean H64−DWC KL / wins | Offset 160: mean H64−DWC KL / wins | Passed both |
|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| 8 | `-0.00067653` / 2 of 8 | `+0.00084696` / 7 of 8 | no |
| 16 | `-0.00101925` / 3 of 8 | `+0.00005847` / 5 of 8 | no |
| 42 | `+0.00009328` / 6 of 8 | `+0.00050326` / 5 of 8 | yes |
| 62 | `+0.00009107` / 3 of 8 | `+0.00044128` / 7 of 8 | no |

Only one of four layers passed both splits; the requirement was at least three. The pooled paired
point estimate was `+0.00004232` nats, but its stratified 95% bootstrap interval was
`[-0.00034679, +0.00041532]`, which includes zero. The maximum recurrence residual was
`3.11e-16`, so the rejection is statistical rather than a replay-integrity failure.

Per the frozen rule, the teacher-uncertainty control, ESS/temperature alternatives, full checkpoint
build, perplexity, divergence-suite, and GSM8K evaluations were not run. This configuration does
not improve the H64 default. The compact machine-readable record is
`benchmarks/qwen3.8-27b-dwc-probe.json`. The transient Spark distributions, Hessians, caches,
and full replay reports were removed after their SHA-256 identities and aggregate gate results
were recorded. No candidate checkpoint existed to retain.

## Resource boundary

- Jobs run detached on Spark so SSH loss does not terminate them.
- Full-vocabulary collection: at most 64 contexts per model.
- One weighted streaming calibration pass; peak residency must remain bounded as in H64.
- Two fixed local replay splits; no candidate sweep.
- Maximum six GPU-hours before automatic stop.
- No full checkpoint rebuild before both local and specific-signal gates pass.

## Novelty boundary

ModelOpt AutoQuant and several public Qwen checkpoints already use gradient or KL sensitivity to
assign modules to NVFP4, FP8, or BF16. AWQ/GPTQ and Local-Hessian methods already change scales or
weights from activation statistics. This probe makes a narrower claim: the measured
quantized-versus-BF16 output disagreement changes the sampling distribution used to recollect the
Hessian, and the process closes one iterative PTQ feedback loop.

The hard-example reweighting principle is not new in machine learning. Until a literature review
and positive held-out result exist, MxWave must not describe DWC as the first or a novel method.
